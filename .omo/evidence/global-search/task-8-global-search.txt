DEVIATIONS RECORDED: none in the contract. One test-side assumption was corrected (STEP 4 note).

`total` DEFINITION (documented choice, per task): the sum of count(results) across the returned groups — i.e. the
number of rows the palette is about to render (each group already capped at 5 by the service), NOT a database count.
Rationale: the typeahead contract carries no counts and must never run a COUNT(*) query; a capped per-group fetch is
the plan's count strategy (line 60) and `has_more` (which would say "5+") is deliberately a full-page key.
